THF is a radiolucent hand and wrist fixation device designed for intraoperative fluoroscopy, holding the patient's hand and wrist stable during imaging without obstructing the X-ray view.

**Recognition:**

- **Excellence in Manufacturing Award**, UC Davis Senior Design, 2021
- Presented at the UC Davis College of Engineering Design Showcase, 2022
