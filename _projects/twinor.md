TwinOR creates photorealistic digital twins of dynamic operating rooms, providing a simulation platform for embodied AI research in interventional care. By reconstructing real ORs with their equipment, staff, and workflow dynamics, TwinOR enables training and evaluation of embodied agents in realistic surgical environments.

Accepted at the *International Journal of Computer Assisted Radiology and Surgery*, 2026 (IPCAI 2026 special issue).
