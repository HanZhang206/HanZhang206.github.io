Neural Digital Twins reconstructs complex medical environments, such as operating rooms, into digital twins that support spatial planning in virtual reality — for example, planning OR layouts and equipment placement before a procedure.

**Recognition:** honored with the **Bench-to-Bedside Award** and audience vote for **long oral** presentation at IPCAI 2024.
