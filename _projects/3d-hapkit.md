3D Hapkit is a low-cost, 3-degree-of-freedom haptic device built on a delta parallel mechanism, extending the classic 1-DOF Hapkit to spatial force feedback for haptics education and prototyping.

**Recognition:**

- Demoed at the **IEEE World Haptics Conference 2023**, Delft, Netherlands
- **Best Project Award**, Haptic Interface Design, Johns Hopkins University, 2023
