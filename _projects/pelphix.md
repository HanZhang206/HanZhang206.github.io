Pelphix is the first approach to surgical phase recognition in X-ray guided surgery, using dynamic simulation to generate training data for recognizing workflow phases of percutaneous pelvis fixation directly from X-ray images.

Published at MICCAI 2023 and featured in the **JHU Hub** and **Surgery International**.
