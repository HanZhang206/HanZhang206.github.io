PelvisVR recreates pelvic trauma surgery in virtual reality, including interventional X-ray imaging, so that surgeons and radiology technicians can cross-train in each other's roles. Our study "Stand in Surgeon's Shoes" shows that VR cross-training enhances teamwork and communication in the operating room.
