StraightTrack is a mixed reality navigation system that guides surgeons during percutaneous K-wire insertion, overlaying trajectory guidance directly in the surgical field to improve accuracy while reducing radiation exposure from repeated fluoroscopy.

Published in *Wiley Healthcare Technology Letters*, 2024 (AE-CAI 2024 special issue).
