I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [Johns Hopkins University](https://www.jhu.edu/), where I am a member of the [Advanced Robotics and Computationally Augmented Environments (ARCADE)](https://arcade.cs.jhu.edu/) research group led by [Prof. Mathias Unberath](https://mathiasunberath.github.io), affiliated with the [Laboratory for Computational Sensing and Robotics](https://lcsr.jhu.edu/).

My research interests focus on leveraging **humanoid and embodied AI** to design the next generation of **surgical automation**. I work on photorealistic digital twins of operating rooms, mixed reality and human-in-the-loop interfaces for interventional care, and surgical workflow understanding.

Before starting my Ph.D., I earned an M.S. in Biomedical Engineering from Johns Hopkins University in 2023 and a B.S. in Biomedical Engineering with Honors from [University of California, Davis](https://bme.ucdavis.edu/) in 2022. In my free time, you can find me hiking and playing badminton.
